Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Hermes ARC is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Caleb Khoo. No one else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission it reads and manages his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts, to sort email, draft replies and keep notes.

Email content is processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) to classify and summarise it. Data is kept on the owner's private server and private files. It is not sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

The owner can withdraw this access at any time from his Google account's security settings.
